- **Milestone 6, E6.9 first slice: investigation of "why" questions (same branch, run locally and live 2026-11-15, CI not run):** [ADR-0010](docs/adr/ADR-0010-bounded-adaptive-investigation.md) option A, implemented in `apps/ai-engine/src/ai_engine/investigation/engine.py`.
